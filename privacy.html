Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026 · SikaSense Ltd, Accra, Ghana · Contact: privacy@sikasense.example.com

1. Who we are

SikaSense provides an AI business-management assistant operated through WhatsApp and a web dashboard for small and medium enterprises in Ghana. This policy explains what we collect and your rights.

2. Information we collect

  • Business & contact data: business name, owner name, WhatsApp number, email.
  • Financial records you create: sales, expenses, debts, stock levels, invoices and customer details you log with the assistant.
  • Voice notes: audio you send is transcribed to text; audio is deleted after transcription (retained max 30 days for quality review, only if you opt in).
  • Transaction data: mobile-money and bank statement data you connect for reconciliation, accessed read-only via authorized APIs.
  • Technical data: usage analytics — only after you accept cookies.

3. How we use it

  • To answer your questions, keep your books, send reminders you request, and generate reports and invoices.
  • To improve transcription and matching accuracy (de-identified where possible).
  • To meet Ghanaian tax, audit and legal obligations.

4. Cookies

Essential cookies make the site work. Analytics load only after you accept the cookie banner. Withdraw anytime by clearing site data.

5. Sharing & security

We never sell your data. We share only the minimum with service providers (hosting, WhatsApp infrastructure, payment providers) under contract. All data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest. AI processing happens on secured servers — your business data is never used to train public models.

6. Your rights

Request access, correction, export or deletion of your data at privacy@sikasense.example.com — we respond within 30 days. You may complain to the Data Protection Commission, Ghana. Deleting your account deletes your records within 90 days, except where tax law requires retention.
